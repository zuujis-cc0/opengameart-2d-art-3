#Spooky Cursors Pack Vol. 1 by Tisroc#
######################################

Thank you for purchasing this pack!

Here you'll find a collection of four spooky-style cursors with their basic variations, ready for use in your projects.
Each cursor has four states: default, hover, click and wait. Stay tuned! More cursors, states, and animations will be added soon.


##Please note:

- All cursors have their hotspot at 5, 5
- All cursors are 64x64 in size
